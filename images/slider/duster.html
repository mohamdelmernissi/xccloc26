The best-selling car in Spain is made in Morocco

Ouardani Issa (     Latifa Babas)
Publié Le 07/10/2025 à 15h20 Temps de lecture: 1'
DR
‹
›

The Dacia Sandero, manufactured in Morocco, continues to dominate car sales in Spain, emerging as the country’s best-selling vehicle in September, according to data from the Spanish automobile market.

Derived from the Logan model, the Sandero is known for being one of the most reliable and economical cars in its category. This combination has fueled its strong demand not only in Spain but also across Europe, where it has consistently ranked among the top-selling vehicles for years.

The Sandero is produced in Morocco as well as in Mioveni, Romania, the home base of the Dacia brand. Founded in 1966, Dacia is now a subsidiary of the French Renault Group.

The company follows a straightforward production philosophy focused on offering practical, affordable cars rather than luxury or high-end technology, a formula that has made the Sandero a popular choice for consumers seeking value and simplicity.
